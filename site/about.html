My process

Four steps, every time. The order matters more than the tools.

1

Research

  • Product research & problem scoping — why was it built, what's the hypothesis?
  • Competitor research — who does it right, who does it wrong (I categorise reviews by severity).
  • User research — interviews, questionnaires pulled into a sheet for linear analysis.
  • Analytics, if any exist yet.
2

Concept

  • Personas — the soul behind the product; everything later is checked against them.
  • Outline — a text version of the app so everyone's on the same page before wireframes.
  • User flows — standard ones, and emotional ones for onboarding and checkout, where things can go wrong.
3

Wireframes

  • Hi-fi wireframes — easier to usability-test and reusable in the UI stage.
  • Clickable prototype in Figma.
  • Usability testing with the people I interviewed and new people who match the personas. Iterate here, not later.
4

UI

  • UI built on the wireframes, inspired by users, competitors, NN/g, games, everyday things.
  • Design system (atomic, light + dark when needed).
  • Design → code: interaction specs prototyped live, then a CSS/Xcode audit so the shipped thing matches.

Values I believe in

Three things I keep coming back to

1

Humans, not users

Data tells you where; empathy tells you why. I need both before I draw anything.

2

Ship fast, validate

A product in people's hands beats a perfect deck. Find product-market fit, then polish.

3

Stay until it's live

Design doesn't end at handoff. I audit the code, the pixels, and the interactions until it ships.
